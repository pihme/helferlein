# Teardrop

Narrow top, round base. A face and two arms.

![Teardrop](teardrop.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The shell comes to a point at the top and swells into a round base. The visor sits on the wide middle. The arms leave the sides and hang beside the round part, each two bones and a round hand.

## Side

The face is on the left. The point and the round base are the same in profile. The visor is a dark disk on the front of the wide part. One arm shows, bent.

## Top

The base is a circle, and the point is a small peak at the center. The visor points down, across the near edge. Both arms stick out left and right.

## Context

The same drop, in three-quarter view. The point stays the top of the figure.
