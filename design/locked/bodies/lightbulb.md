# Lightbulb

A glass bulb over a straight socket, with a filament inside. A face and two arms.

![Lightbulb](lightbulb.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The glass is a tall bulb, pointed at the crown and clear enough to show a pale coiled filament behind the visor. Under the glass is a short straight socket with a screw thread, and a small contact at the bottom. The visor sits on the glass. The arms leave beside the lower glass and hang out, each two bones and a round hand.

## Side

The face is on the left. The bulb, the threaded socket, and the contact read in profile. The filament shows as a small ring inside the glass, behind the visor. One arm shows, bent, beside the bulb.

## Top

The glass is a circle. The filament is a wavy line across the inside. The visor points down. The socket is hidden under the glass. Both arms stick out left and right.

## Context

The same bulb, in three-quarter view. The thread and the contact stay visible under the glass, and the filament still shows through it.
