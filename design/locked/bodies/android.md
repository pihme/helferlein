# Android

Tall head, small torso. One smooth shell, with a face and two arms. This is the reference body.

![Android](android.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The shell is one piece: a pointed crown, a tall head, a soft waist, and a rounder lower body. A dark visor sits on the head, with two light eyes and a small smile. Two arms leave the shoulders, each an upper and a forearm ending in a round hand, and hang out to the sides.

## Side

The face is on the left. The visor is a dark disk on the front of the head, and the eyes show just past it. The crown rises to a soft point. The back is a continuous curve down to a rounded base. One arm shows in profile, bent, with the hand low.

## Top

The shell is nearly round. The face points down: the visor is the dark oval at the bottom, and the eyes sit just past it. Both arms leave left and right, bent, with the hands as small balls.

## Context

The same figure, from the stage camera, in three-quarter view. The tall head, the visor, and both arms read together. Nothing is worn and nothing is held.
