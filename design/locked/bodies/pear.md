# Pear

Small head, wide torso. No stem and no leaf. A face and two arms.

![Pear](pear.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The head is a small bulb on a wide round belly. The visor fills most of the head. The arms leave where the head meets the belly and hang beside the wide part, each two bones and a round hand.

## Side

The face is on the left. The head is a small lobe on the front-top of the big belly, and the visor sits on that lobe. The belly is the whole lower mass. One arm shows in profile against it.

## Top

The belly is a large circle. The head is a smaller circle inside it, toward the middle. The visor points down, on the near side of the head. Both arms stick out left and right.

## Context

The same figure, in three-quarter view. The small head and the wide belly are the whole read.
