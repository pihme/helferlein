# Egg

One rounded capsule. A face and two arms.

![Egg](egg.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

A single tall oval, a little wider through the middle, with no waist and no separate head. The visor sits on the upper half. The arms leave the sides of the oval and hang down, each two bones and a round hand.

## Side

The face is on the left. The profile is the same tall oval. The visor is a dark disk on the front, and the eyes show just past it. One arm shows, bent, in front of the shell.

## Top

The shell is round. The visor points down. Both arms stick out left and right, bent.

## Context

The same capsule, in three-quarter view. One outline from crown to base.
