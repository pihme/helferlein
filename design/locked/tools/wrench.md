# Wrench

An open jaw on a straight handle. In the solo views the jaw points up.

![Wrench](wrench.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the wrench was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The handle is a plain rod. The jaw is one open C at the top, with the gap facing up.

## Side

The wrench is thin. The handle is a line, and the jaw is a small notch at the top.

## Top

Looking along the handle, the wrench is a long flat bar. The open jaw is the forked end.

## Context

The forearm lies level, out to the screen-right, and the elbow bends back. The wrench stands up from the fist, and the open jaw faces the viewer.
