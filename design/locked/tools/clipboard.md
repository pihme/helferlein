# Clipboard

A board, a pale page, and a clip. In the solo views the clip points up.

![Clipboard](clipboard.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the clipboard was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

A tall rectangle. A darker frame holds a lighter page. The clip is a wide bar across the top of the page, with a small raised tab at its center.

## Side

The board is a thin slab. The clip is the part that stands proud of the page, at the top. The page reads as a second thin layer on the board.

## Top

Looking down, the board is a long thin bar. The clip is a shorter block across the middle of that bar.

## Context

The right hand holds the lower edge, on the screen-right of the figure. The board stands upright, the page faces the viewer, and the clip stays at the top.
