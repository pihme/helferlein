# Hammer

A handle and a head. One end of the head is slanted. In the solo views the head points up.

![Hammer](hammer.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the hammer was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The handle is a straight rod. The head sits across the top of it. One end of the head is square, and the other end slopes down to a blunt point.

## Side

The handle is a thin rod. The head is a short block at the top, only a little wider than the handle, because the length of the head runs across the view.

## Top

Looking along the handle, the head is a long bar. A lighter band marks where the handle meets it.

## Context

The right arm reaches out to the screen-right. The fist is on the handle, and the head is beyond the fist.
