# Tongs

Two arms joined in a bend, with a pad on each tip. In the solo views the tips point up.

![Tongs](tongs.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the tongs were built in. Context is the android holding them. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The arms form a narrow V. They meet in a round bend at the bottom and end in small pads at the top. The opening stays open.

## Side

The two arms lie on top of each other, so the tongs read as one rod. A pad shows at the top, and the bend shows at the bottom.

## Top

Looking along the arms, both pads show, one at each end of a bar. The bend is the middle of that bar.

## Context

The forearm points forward. The fist holds the bend, and the tips point down toward a spot in front of the body.
