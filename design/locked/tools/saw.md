# Saw

A blade with teeth along one edge, and a round handle. In the solo views the blade points up.

![Saw](saw.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the saw was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The handle is a ring at the bottom. The blade is a long flat bar above it. The teeth run along one edge of the blade, up to the tip.

## Side

The blade is thin, so the teeth read as a serrated line. The handle is a small loop at the bottom, seen edge on.

## Top

Looking along the blade, the saw is a long rounded bar. A dark gap in that bar is the hole through the handle.

## Context

The fist holds the outer rim of the ring. The blade points forward and in, in front of the body, and the teeth face down.
