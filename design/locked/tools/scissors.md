# Scissors

Two blades, a pivot, and two bows. In the solo views the blades point up, and they are open.

![Scissors](scissors.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the scissors were built in. Context is the android holding them. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The blades cross at a round pivot and spread toward the tips. Below the pivot, a short shank leads to two separate rings. The rings do not touch.

## Side

The scissors are thin. The two blades lie almost on top of each other, so they read as one line with a split near the tips. The pivot is a ball. The two rings overlap into one loop.

## Top

Looking along the blades, they stick out to either side of the pivot ball. The rings are the shorter shapes on the near side of the ball.

## Context

The right arm reaches out to the screen-right. The fist is at the rings, and the open blades point past the hand.
