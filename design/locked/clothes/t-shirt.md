# T-shirt

A plain shirt with a simple neckline. The cloth is a drawing on the body, not a separate shape.

![T-shirt](t-shirt.png)

Front, side, and top are the cloth on the shell, with no face and no arms. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No tool or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The head stays bare. One flat blue covers the torso from a neckline that dips slightly at the center. No collar, stripe, or button.

## Side

The front is on the left. The neckline is lower at the front and rises toward the back. The blue is the same all the way around. The bare head is the upper curve.

## Top

Looking down, the bare crown fills the panel. The shirt is hidden under the head.

## Context

On the android, the plain blue sits under the visor. The arms stay bare.
