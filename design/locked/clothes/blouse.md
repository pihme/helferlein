# Blouse

A scooped neck, a floral print, and a bow at the throat. The cloth is a drawing on the body, not a separate shape.

![Blouse](blouse.png)

Front, side, and top are the cloth on the shell, with no face and no arms. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No tool or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The head stays bare. The blouse covers the torso from a low scoop. A small bow sits at the center of the neckline. Pale flowers, each with a darker center, scatter over the blue ground and stop short of the scoop.

## Side

The front is on the left. The scoop is lower toward the front and rises toward the back. The bow sticks out a little at the front of the neck. The flowers wrap onto the side. The bare head is the whole upper curve.

## Top

Looking down, the bare crown fills the panel. The blouse is hidden under the head, so no flowers show.

## Context
