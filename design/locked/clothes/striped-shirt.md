# Striped shirt

Horizontal stripes. The cloth is a drawing on the body, not a separate shape.

![Striped shirt](striped-shirt.png)

Front, side, and top are the cloth on the shell, with no face and no arms. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No tool or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The head stays bare. The shirt covers the torso in even horizontal bands of two blues, from a neckline that dips a little at the center down to the base.

## Side

The front is on the left. The bands continue around the side without a break. The neckline is lower at the front and higher toward the back. The bare head is the upper curve.

## Top

Looking down, the bare crown fills the panel. The stripes are hidden under the head.

## Context

On the android, the bands wrap the torso under the visor. The arms stay bare.
