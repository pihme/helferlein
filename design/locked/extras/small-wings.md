# Small wings

An upper pair and a lower pair. The upper wings are the larger ones.

![Small wings](small-wings.png)

Front, side, and top show the wings on their own. Context wears them on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

Four ovals. On each side, a large wing sits above a smaller, paler wing. The large wings stand taller than the small ones.

## Side

Each pair stacks along the view, so one large oval and one small oval fill the panel. Both are thin.

## Top

The wings are flat slivers, two shapes set apart. They are much longer than they are thick.

## Context

The wings sit on the back. The upper pair shows beside the shoulders, and the lower pair shows lower on the back. They stay spread, out to the sides.
