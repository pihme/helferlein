# Propeller

Three blades on a round hub.

![Propeller](propeller.png)

Front, side, and top show the propeller on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

The hub is a ball. Two blades show, leaving opposite sides of the hub. The third blade lies along the view, so it hides in the hub.

## Side

The same hub, with a blade to each side. A pale patch on the hub is the end of the blade that points at the viewer.

## Top

All three blades show, evenly spaced around the hub.

## Context

The hub sits on top of the head, and the three blades lie across the crown.
