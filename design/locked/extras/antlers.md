# Antlers

Two racks. The tines fan apart, and the two sides do not meet.

![Antlers](antlers.png)

Front, side, and top show the antlers on their own. Context wears them on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

A rack on the left and a rack on the right, with a clear gap between them. Each beam leaves a thick base, rises, and splits into tines. The tines point up and out, and they do not cross the other rack.

## Side

The racks are flat, so from the side they stack into one tall thin shape. The base is the wide end at the bottom, and the tines run up that edge.

## Top

Each rack is a flat wedge. They point away from each other, left and right, and stay thin.

## Context

The racks sit on the head, above the visor. The tips stay apart over the crown.
