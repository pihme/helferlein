# Sombrero

A very wide brim, turned up at the edge, and a low rounded crown.

![Sombrero](sombrero.png)

Front, side, and top show the hat on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

The brim is a long shallow dish, the edge lifted. A low dome sits at the center. The brim is many times wider than the crown is tall.

## Side

The same dish and the same dome. The brim's edge turns up at both ends. The crown stays a low bump.

## Top

A large disk. The crown is a smaller dome at the center, and the brim fills the rest.

## Context

The crown sits on the head, and the brim spreads well past the head on both sides. The visor stays visible under the brim.
