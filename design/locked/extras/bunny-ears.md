# Bunny ears

Two long ears. They stand up from the head when worn.

![Bunny ears](bunny-ears.png)

Front, side, and top show the ears on their own. Context wears them on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

Two long ovals, side by side, each rounded at both ends. They lean apart a little. No inner marking.

## Side

The two ears lie along the same view, so one tall oval fills the panel. It is long and narrow.

## Top

Each ear is a small flat oval. They sit apart, left and right.

## Context

The ears rise from the top of the head, above the visor, and lean apart. They are much taller than the visor.
