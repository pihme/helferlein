# Dog ears

A rounded paddle on each cheek, with a darker inside.

![Dog ears](dog-ears.png)

Front, side, and top show the ears on their own. Context wears them on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

Two short paddles, side by side and far apart, so each one is small in the panel. Each paddle is a rounded lobe with a darker inner patch.

## Side

The paddles lie along the view, so one fills the panel. It is a tall rounded lobe, darker on the front edge, with the inner patch on the face of the lobe.

## Top

Each ear is a thin flat oval. They sit apart, left and right, and they are much wider than they are thick.

## Context

The paddles sit on the cheeks, beside the visor, not on top of the head. The darker inside faces forward. They do not hang to the shoulders.
