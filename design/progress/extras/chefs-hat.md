# Chef's hat

A band and a tall puff with a flattened top.

![Chef's hat](chefs-hat.png)

Front, side, and top show the hat on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

A straight band holds a wide puff. The puff bulges past the band and flattens on top, with a soft peak at the center of that flat.

## Side

The same band and the same puff. The puff is as deep as it is wide, and the top stays flattened.

## Top

The puff is a circle. A small mark at the center is the peak.

## Context

The band sits on the head, and the puff rises above it. The visor stays clear below the band.
