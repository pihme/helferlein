# Beret

A soft low cap with a small stem. In the solo views it lies level. Worn, it tilts over the forehead.

![Beret](beret.png)

Front, side, and top show the beret on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

A wide low dome, flat on the bottom, with a small stem on top. The edge is a soft rim, not a stiff brim.

## Side

The same low dome. The stem is a nub on the crown. The beret is much wider than it is tall.

## Top

A round disk. The stem is a small bump at the center.

## Context

The beret sits on the head, tilted down over the forehead. The stem is on the high side, toward the back of the crown.
