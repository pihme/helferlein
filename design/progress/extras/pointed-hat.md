# Pointed hat

A wide brim and a cone with a ball at the tip. The tip points back.

![Pointed hat](pointed-hat.png)

Front, side, and top show the hat on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

The cone stands on a wide flat brim and ends in a small ball. From the front the cone looks straight, because the lean is toward the back.

## Side

The front is on the left. The cone leaves the brim and bends up toward the right, which is the back. The ball is at that tip. The brim stays a thin slab.

## Top

The brim is a wide disk. The cone is a smaller shape inside it, and the ball marks the tip, set back from the center.

## Context

The brim sits on the head. The cone rises and the tip points back, past the crown.
