# Crown

An open band with five points. The head shows through.

![Crown](crown.png)

Front, side, and top show the crown on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

A low band carries five points. The middle point is the tallest. A shorter point sits to each side of it, and a taller point stands at each end.

## Side

The band is a low strip. Points stick up along it: a tall one at the far end, the middle point, and another toward the near end. The band is open, so there is no cap.

## Top

A ring. The five points are small peaks around the ring. The middle is empty.

## Context

The band sits on the head, and the points rise around the crown. The visor and the head show through the ring.
