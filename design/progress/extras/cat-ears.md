# Cat ears

Two pointed ears with a darker inside. They sit on the front of the head when worn.

![Cat ears](cat-ears.png)

Front, side, and top show the ears on their own. Context wears them on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

Two triangles, side by side. Each has a pale rim and a darker inner triangle. The tips are rounded a little, and the bases are open.

## Side

The ears stack along the view, so one tall triangle fills the panel. The dark inside is the whole middle of that triangle.

## Top

Each ear is a fan, wide at the opening and narrow at the tip. The two fans sit apart.

## Context

The ears stand on the front of the head, just above the visor, one to each side. The tips point up, and the dark insides face forward.
