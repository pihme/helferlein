# Halo

A thin ring. In the solo views it lies level. Worn, it floats above the head.

![Halo](halo.png)

Front, side, and top show the halo on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

The ring is edge on, so it reads as a long rounded bar, a little thicker through the middle.

## Side

The same bar. The ring has no gap and no upright part in this view.

## Top

A full circle, thin, with an empty center.

## Context

The ring floats level above the head, clear of the crown. It does not touch the visor.
