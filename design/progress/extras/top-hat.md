# Top hat

A tall straight crown and a brim that turns up at the edge. A darker band marks the join.

![Top hat](top-hat.png)

Front, side, and top show the hat on its own. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No clothes or tool. Hue 210.

## Front

The crown is a straight cylinder. The brim flares out and up at the ends. A darker band circles the crown where it meets the brim.

## Side

The crown is the same straight tube. The brim is a shallow curve, higher at the ends than in the middle. The band is the dark line at the join.

## Top

The brim is a wide oval. The crown is a ring inside it, open at the center.

## Context

The crown stands on the head, and the brim flares around it. The band shows at the bottom of the crown.
