# Turtleneck

A high ribbed collar over a plain body. The cloth is a drawing on the body, not a separate shape.

![Turtleneck](turtleneck.png)

Front, side, and top are the cloth on the shell, with no face and no arms. Context wears it on the android. Neutral expression. No tool or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

The head stays bare. Several narrow horizontal ribs form a collar, higher than the other shirts. Below the ribs the torso is one flat blue, down to the base.

## Side

The front is on the left. The ribs wrap the neck as the same narrow bands and sit close under the bare head. The plain blue continues around the torso.

## Top

Looking down, the bare crown fills the panel. The collar is hidden under the head.

## Context

On the android, the ribbed collar sits just under the visor, and the plain blue covers the torso. The arms stay bare.
