# Brush

A handle, a ferrule, and bristles that come to a sharp tip. In the solo views the tip points up.

![Brush](brush.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the brush was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

A straight handle, a short ferrule a little wider than the handle, then the bristles. The bristles are fullest beside the ferrule and hollow inward to a sharp point. They stay narrower than the ferrule.

## Side

The same profile as the front. The brush is round, so the tip, the swell, and the handle read the same from the side.

## Top

Looking down the tip, the brush is a circle. The dark center is the point, and the wider ring is the bristles around it.

## Context

The right hand holds the handle low, on the screen-right of the figure. The tip points down.
