# Telescope

Two tubes. The wider one ends in a lip, and the corner between the tubes is smooth. In the solo views the wide end points up.

![Telescope](telescope.png)

Front, side, and top use the axes the telescope was built in. Context is the android holding it. Neutral expression. No clothes or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

A lip rims the wide end. That tube narrows and meets the slimmer tube in a smoothed corner, not a hard step. A short narrower end finishes the slim tube.

## Side

The same two tubes and the same lip. The silhouette matches the front, so the barrel is round.

## Top

Looking down the wide end, the telescope is one circle, the lip.

## Context

The right arm is raised on the screen-right of the figure. The hand holds the slim tube, and the wide end points out beside the head.
