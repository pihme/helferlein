# Snowman

Three stacked spheres. The lowest is the largest. A face and two arms.

![Snowman](snowman.png)

Front, side, top, and context of the current look. A body is the whole figure. Neutral expression. No clothes, tool, or extra. Hue 210.

## Front

Three spheres, touching: a small head, a middle ball, and a large base. The visor is on the head. The arms leave the middle ball and hang beside it, each two bones and a round hand.

## Side

The face is on the left. The three spheres stack in the same order, the base the widest. The visor is a dark disk on the front of the head. One arm shows against the middle ball.

## Top

Three circles, one inside the next: the base, the middle, and the head. The visor points down, on the head. Both arms stick out from the middle circle, left and right.

## Context

The same stack, in three-quarter view. The three sizes stay distinct.
