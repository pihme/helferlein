# Helferlein

An animated 3D avatar. A little helper you can see.

A host calls it into view. It swoops in, idles, and shows when it is working. When the work shows up on the page, it spins, and a new look appears while its back faces you. When it is dismissed, it folds back into a small resting form.

It starts as a plain android, with no clothes, no tool, and no extra. A new look changes the color and one or two of the other parts, not every part. The new color is far enough from the old one to read as different. Something that just changed sits out the next look. A new tool, new clothes, or a new extra stays for a while, and a rare extra stays longer. Putting a piece on is the common change. Taking it off is the uncommon one. Those parts are the body, the expression, the clothes, a tool in hand, and one extra such as bunny ears or a hat. The colors of the parts are chosen together so they harmonize.

The drawing is not settled. Reference sketches, not a design: [sheet](assets/helferlein-poses.svg), [resting](assets/helferlein-lamp.svg), [idle](assets/helferlein-idle.svg), [thinking](assets/helferlein-thinking.svg), [applied](assets/helferlein-applied.svg). Those sketches show the shape of the objects. They do not show how a tool is held.

A demo in this repo has two pages under one navigation bar. The demo page explains the avatar. Its resting figure sits in the bottom right. Opening it shows a chat inset from the corner, and the figure centers above that chat. Sending a message makes the figure work and answer, then reload while it faces away, with a new look and a new background. Wardrobe puts body, face, and color on the left of the figure, and clothes, tool, and extra on the right. A button under the figure runs one roll, and choosing a part plays the same turn.
